More Than Just Food

Japan combines ancient traditions, modern cities, beautiful landscapes and a food culture unlike anywhere else.

A country of contrasts.

Japan is a place where traditional temples can exist alongside huge skyscrapers and futuristic technology.

This contrast can also be seen in Japanese food. Traditional recipes have been passed down for generations, while chefs continue to create new and exciting dishes.

Food in Japan is not only about taste. Presentation, texture, freshness and seasonality are all important.

Why Is Japanese Food So Amazing?

Japanese cuisine focuses on details that make even simple ingredients taste special.

01

Fresh Ingredients

Fresh fish, vegetables, rice and other ingredients are carefully selected to create high-quality dishes.

02

Perfect Balance

Japanese cooking often balances different flavours, textures and temperatures instead of relying on just one.

03

Beautiful Presentation

The appearance of food matters. Colours, shapes and arrangement can make a meal feel like artwork.

04

Huge Variety

Japan has everything from sushi and ramen to street food, desserts, grilled dishes and regional specialities.

05

Tradition

Many Japanese dishes have long histories and traditions that have been passed from generation to generation.

06

Seasonality

Japanese cuisine often changes with the seasons, using ingredients when they are at their best.

Food Is Part of the Experience

In Japan, eating can be about much more than satisfying hunger. Restaurants, markets, festivals and family meals all create different experiences. From eating ramen in a busy Tokyo neighbourhood to enjoying traditional food in Kyoto, Japanese cuisine gives you another way to experience the culture.
